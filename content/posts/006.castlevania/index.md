Yesterday, I'd look for some retro device with decent peripherals, which can be programmed on rust. I discovered GBA and `agb` library which is very up to date. So, I tried this and suprisingly, it was very easy comparing to [PSX](https://github.com/mishazawa/microsexual) or [NDS](https://github.com/mishazawa/ndslife). Yeas, library is high level, but still it was very satisfying experience.

Btw, what is fascinating me - is how interesting was design that days and how it is shape the process of programming. How it is stimulate creativity with constraints.

Later, I've read interview with developers of Castlevania (GBA parts) and it reminds me when I was a kid and I played Castlevania on Game boy, on the balcony, in Crimea with my brother. We barter 2 cartridges with boy in Luhansk. From our side it was Prince of Persia and Worms. From his side: Need for speed and Castlevania. Afaik, he told us that it is a good game, but we don't like it for the time being. I remember, that I played NFS a lot, and it was 3D-ish, but very quickly became boring. But later, my brother discovered that it was really GOOD game. Interesting, how such a random events can influence a life...
